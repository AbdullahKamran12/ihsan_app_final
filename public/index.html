Turn any TV into a
professional prayer board

The Ihsan Display Screen is a dedicated full-screen mode built for mosque TVs and projectors. Fully linked with the app — every change you make is live on screen instantly.

Adhan & Iqamah alerts — automatic audio & visual alerts at each prayer, plus dua after Adhan
Jumu'ah khutbah mode — dedicated Friday display with multiple Jumu'ah times and khutbah countdown
Full offline support — upload a full year's timetable and it runs forever, even with no WiFi
Post-Salah dhikr — automatically shows guided dhikr after each prayer for the congregation
Portrait & landscape modes — fits any screen, fully customisable with your photos & Islamic patterns
