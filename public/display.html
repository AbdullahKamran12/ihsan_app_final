Stored as jummahTimes array on each Friday's prayerTimes document. The display app reads up to 4 slots (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Jumu'ah).

After uploading, the TV display will reload the background at next startup or needsRefresh trigger. Use a landscape image (16:9) for best results. The app uses BackgroundPattern.customImage guard — if you remove the image, the display reverts to its pattern/style setting.
